What Bachelor degrees are offered at EM Normandie Dubai?+

EM Normandie Dubai offers four specialized undergraduate programs: (1) Bachelor in Management, (2) Bachelor Management – Digital Business, (3) Bachelor Management – Logistics and Port Management, and (4) Bachelor Management – Services Industries. All degrees are 3 years in duration and taught in English.

Are the Bachelor degrees accredited in the UAE and internationally?+

Yes. EM Normandie in Dubai is officially licensed and accredited by the UAE Ministry of Education through the Commission for Academic Accreditation (CAA) and recognized in France by the Ministry of Higher Education through the CEFDG (Commission d'Évaluation des Formations et Diplômes de Gestion). Furthermore, the school holds the rare international "Triple Crown" accreditations (EQUIS, AACSB, and AMBA), placing it in the top 1% of business schools worldwide.

Is study mobility to France mandatory during the Bachelor?+

Yes! An integral hallmark of EM Normandie is guaranteed international exposure. Bachelor students undertake mandatory mobility at EM Normandie's French campuses (such as Paris or Le Havre) or Oxford (UK), allowing you to experience global business firsthand while completing your degree.

How does the mandatory 4-month internship work?+

Students complete a structured 4-month professional internship within reputable companies in Dubai, the UAE, or internationally. The EM Normandie Career Center actively supports students with corporate connections, interview coaching, and career development workshops.

What are the entry criteria for high school graduates?+

Applicants should possess a recognised High School Diploma (Tawjihiyya, IB, British A-Levels, American Diploma, French Baccalaureate, CBSE/ISC, or equivalent) with competitive academic grades. Applicants must also demonstrate English proficiency (e.g., IELTS 5.5+, TOEFL 70+, EmSAT 1250+, or high school taught in English) and attend an admissions interview.

Does EM Normandie provide UAE student visas?+

Yes, EM Normandie Dubai assists international and local full-time students with UAE student visa sponsorship throughout the duration of their degree.
